# Ashen Bond: dialogue script and voice guide

Every line the game speaks, in order of play. "Narrator" lines show in italics on screen. Line IDs (A-01 …) are
for naming the recorded files: `A-01_narrator.wav`, `A-02_ilyan.wav`, and so on.

## The voices

| Character | Who | Voice direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Ilyan** | The boy, about 9 to 11 years old | Young, breathy, scared but stubborn. He speaks softly and gets louder only when he calls for Mira. He isn't a hero yet, so he shouldn't sound like one. Small pauses, a crack in the voice on the guilty lines (B-12, B-13). |
| **Mira** | His older sister's spirit, about 16 | Warm, calm, protective, a big-sister tone with a little teasing humour. Speak low and close to the mic, almost a whisper, with a faint airy echo or reverb, as if she's slightly far away. She never sounds sad when she comforts him. |
| **Narrator** | Storybook voice | Neutral, gentle, older (any gender), slow pace. Like reading a bedtime story about something terrible. No drama in the delivery: let the words carry it. |
| **The Executioner** | Area B boss | Very deep, slow, cold, cruel but quiet. A rasp, as if the voice comes from inside armour. Pitch it down and add a light distortion. |
| **The Clockwork Eye** | Area C boss | Whispering, many voices at once, mechanical. Record it twice and layer it with a slight delay; add a soft ticking under it. |
| **Enemy taunts** | Every small enemy | Growly, hissing, short. One actor can do all of them with pitch shifts. |

Recording tips:
- Mono, 44.1 kHz, about 30 cm from the mic, no music.
- Keep half a second of silence before and after each line.
- Record 2 or 3 takes of each line.

## Area A: The Burning Village

**Opening (plays when the level starts)**
- A-01 Narrator: The village is burning. The great bell has stopped.
- A-02 Ilyan: Mira...? Mira! Where are you? *(calling out, panicked, coughing from the smoke)*
- A-03 Narrator: The lantern is warm in his hands. Something tall and dark rises out of his shadow.
- A-04 Ilyan: Stay away from me! ...Why won't it hurt me? *(scared at first, then confused, almost a whisper)*

**Tutorial: the first enemy** (the camera shows the ember rat)
- A-05 Narrator: An ember beast. Shoot it with the lantern (J). Hold J to charge a stronger flare.
- A-06 Narrator: A burning enemy glows cyan for a moment. Press P and Mira's shadow strike finishes it.

**Tutorial: the broken path** (the camera shows the cracked tree)
- A-07 Narrator: The path is broken. The burning gap is far too wide to jump.
- A-08 Narrator: Shoot the cracked tree three times (J). It will fall across the gap and become your path.

**Memory 1** (near the altar)
- A-09 Narrator: A memory: Mira kneels, closing his small hands around the lantern.
- A-10 Mira: If the bell ever stops, carry the light to the clock tower. Promise me. *(gentle, serious)*
- A-11 Ilyan: ...I promised. *(very quiet, to himself)*

**Before the square ambush**
- A-12 Narrator: Ember beasts crawl out of the ash of the square.
- A-13 Ilyan: There are so many... I can't go back.
- A-14 Mira: Light them up, little brother. I'll do the rest. *(confident, a small smile in the voice)*
- A-15 Ilyan: That voice... no. It can't be. *(stunned, breathless)*

**Leaving the village**
- A-16 Ilyan: The forest. She said the lantern always knows the way to the tower.
- A-17 Narrator: Behind him, the shadow lowers one long hand between the boy and the flames.

## Area B: The Dark Forest

**Opening**
- B-01 Narrator: The Dark Forest. The fire never reached it. Only the shadows did.
- B-02 Ilyan: You followed me. You always follow me. *(half annoyed, half relieved)*
- B-03 Mira: Someone has to keep you out of trouble. *(teasing, warm)*
- B-04 Ilyan: ...Mira? Is it really you? *(hopeful, afraid of the answer)*
- B-05 Narrator: The shadow does not answer. The fireflies drift closer.

**Before the long-walk ambush**
- B-06 Narrator: Hollow shapes rise out of the black earth.
- B-07 Ilyan: They look like they're made of my own shadow.
- B-08 Mira: Fire shows them. I finish them. Together, Ilyan.

**Memory 2** (on the ledge before the black water)
- B-09 Narrator: Another memory: dry cloth in the storeroom. The lantern slipping from his fingers.
- B-10 Narrator: The flame spreading faster than he could scream.
- B-11 Ilyan: I dropped it. It was me. It was always me. *(breaking, near tears: the most important line)*
- B-12 Mira: I know. I came back for you anyway. *(no blame at all, soft, steady)*

**Tutorial: the black water** (the camera shows the dead tree)
- B-13 Narrator: The black water is too wide, even for a double jump.
- B-14 Narrator: Shoot the cracked dead tree three times (J). It will fall and become your bridge.

**Boss: The Executioner**
- B-15 Narrator: In the clearing something old is waiting. A blade made for endings.
- B-16 The Executioner: You dropped the lantern, boy. You remember. You always remember. *(slow, savouring it)*
- B-17 Ilyan: ...It was an accident. *(small, defensive)*
- B-18 The Executioner: Tell that to the fire.
- B-19 Mira: Don't listen to it. Stay behind me, Ilyan. Not this time. *(firm, protective, the first time she sounds angry)*

**Leaving the forest**
- B-20 Ilyan: The tower. The clock stopped at one minute to midnight... the minute I dropped it.
- B-21 Mira: Then let's go and see what is waiting at the top.

## Area C: The Clock Tower

**Opening**
- C-01 Narrator: The Clock Tower. Every gear inside is frozen at 11:59.
- C-02 Ilyan: If I turn the clock back... maybe none of it ever happened. *(desperate hope)*
- C-03 Mira: Time doesn't forgive, Ilyan. But it remembers. *(kind but honest)*
- C-04 Narrator: The tower lends him a little of its power. Hold Q, and the last moments run backwards.

**Tutorial: the crushers** (the camera shows the first crusher)
- C-05 Narrator: The tower's crushers slam on the beat of the clock.
- C-06 Narrator: Wait for the red glow to fade as it rises, then run under it. Caught? Hold Q to rewind.

**Boss: The Clockwork Eye (then the Horned Beast)**
- C-07 Narrator: At the top of the tower, the clockwork opens one great eye.
- C-08 Ilyan: It's watching me. It has been watching all night.
- C-09 The Clockwork Eye: Every second you replay. Every second you regret. *(layered whisper)*
- C-10 Mira: Then we will give it something new to watch.
- C-11 Narrator: Far below, something heavy and horned begins to climb.

**Leaving the tower**
- C-12 Ilyan: It's quiet now. The clock is still stuck.
- C-13 Mira: Not for long. One last door, Ilyan. I'm right here.

## Enemy taunts (one at random, above an enemy when it spots Ilyan)
1. Run, little ember...
2. Your light won't save you.
3. The fire took everything. You're next.
4. I smell guilt on you, boy.
5. That lantern is all you have left.
6. The clock will never move again.
7. She can't protect you forever.
8. Burn with the village!
9. Ashes. All of it, ashes.
10. Turn back, Ilyan.

## Loading screen lines (text only)
1. Mira always said the lantern was heavier than it looked.
2. The bell stopped at one minute to midnight.
3. Fire exposes them. Her shadow finishes them.
4. Hold Q and the world remembers the last few seconds.
5. A shadow that never hurts you is not a monster.
6. Ember beasts flee when they cannot reach you.
7. Carry the light to the clock tower, Ilyan.

When the voice files are ready, name them by line ID (for example `B-11_ilyan.wav`) and put them in
`Assets/Audio/Voice/`. Each line can then play with its subtitle.
